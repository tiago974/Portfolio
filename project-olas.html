OLAS ART — Studio Management Platform

Full-stack interactive studio management web application featuring multi-language localization (EN, ES, UK), real-time attendance rosters, and serverless Google Sheets database synchronization.

Timeline

2026

Independent Project

Role

Full-Stack Developer

UI/UX & Backend Logic

Technologies & Architecture

Google Apps Script HTML5 / Tailwind CSS Vanilla JavaScript (ES6) Google Sheets Database Multi-Language (EN/ES/UK)

Problem & Architectural Solution

Enterprise power without enterprise infrastructure overhead.

Art studios and community cultural centers often struggle with expensive, inflexible SaaS platforms that require steep monthly fees and complicate simple day-to-day operations. Staff members frequently revert to spreadsheets, resulting in disconnected attendance lists, unpaid balances, and supply shortages.

OLAS ART bridges this gap by turning Google Sheets into an instantaneous, multi-user relational datastore fronted by a modern, responsive web application served via Google Apps Script (HtmlService).

The system supports full tri-lingual localization (English, Spanish, and Ukrainian), enabling instructors, administrators, and diverse international students to interact comfortably. Critical workflows—such as marking students 'Paid' or 'Pending', scheduling studio easels, reserving materials, and generating promotional social media captions—are executed in seconds with zero latency.

MODULE // 02

Participants & Student Roster

Real-Time Attendance & Payment Switches

Instructors can select any workshop from the dropdown to access an instant attendance list. The view features reactive toggle switches allowing staff to toggle payment status ('Paid' vs 'Payment Pending') with a single click, instantly writing the transaction update back to the connected Google Sheet.
