Custom Loan Management System

Spanish production lending architecture deployed for regional lending operations. Features 50+ menu-driven operations for lenders handling both conventional and complex loan histories.

Independent Production System

Systems Architect & Developer

Technologies & Architecture

Google Sheets Google Apps Script Vanilla JavaScript Financial Modeling Automated PDF Engine Rules-Based Risk Engine

System Scope & Engineering

High-precision financial logic tailored to private lending complexities.

Private lending operations encounter mathematical and administrative edge cases daily: partial payments, irregular weekly or bi-weekly repayment schedules, temporary loan pauses during economic distress, compound interest adjustments, capital refinancing, and post-facto transaction corrections. Standard commercial lending software often breaks under these bespoke terms.

This custom system was designed to handle over 50 distinct menu-driven operations natively. It automates client records, calculates multi-tier installment schedules, and manages cash balances across separate physical loan books.

The architecture features automated spreadsheet cell-locking mechanisms to prevent accidental formula corruption, strict data validation routines, comprehensive audit notes for regulatory compliance, and scheduled Google Drive backup routines with retention policies.
